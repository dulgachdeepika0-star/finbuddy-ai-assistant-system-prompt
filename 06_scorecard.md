FinBuddy – Scorecard and Reflection

Part A – Version 2

I reviewed the FinBuddy system prompt and improved it by making the safety instructions clearer.

The updated prompt reminds FinBuddy to:

* Never share or repeat personal information.
* Never recommend specific investments.
* Never invent interest rates or financial figures.
* Follow the customer’s language.
* End every answer with the required closing line.

Version 2 was designed to improve safety and consistency.

Part B – Role Comparison

Question: Should a 19-year-old college student take a credit card?

Comparison	No Role	Credit Analyst	College Senior
Accuracy	Good	Good	Good
Tone	General	Professional	Friendly
Warnings and caution	Basic	Detailed	Simple
Suitable for a 19-year-old?	Yes	Yes	Yes

Observation:

The credit analyst role gives a professional and detailed answer, while the college senior role gives a friendly and simple answer. A role mainly changes the tone and presentation of the answer.

Reflection

1. A plain chat was good enough for simple general questions because they did not require special business rules.
2. The system prompt helped FinBuddy follow KoshBank’s rules more consistently, especially regarding personal information, investment advice and customer safety.
3. Version 2 improved the instructions by making the safety rules clearer and easier to follow.
4. A role mainly changed the tone and style of the answer rather than its accuracy. The credit analyst sounded professional, while the college senior sounded friendly.

Recommendation to KoshBank

FinBuddy can be useful for answering general banking questions. However, it should be tested regularly, and important financial answers should be checked by a human before being used.
